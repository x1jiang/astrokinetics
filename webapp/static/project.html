Offline-first astronaut health monitoring when Houston is 44 minutes away

AstroKinetics · ASTRA is an open-source (MIT) web research prototype built in Houston for the NASA Space Apps challenge Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions. It gathers movement, wearable, and habitat signals locally, checks eight emergency red flags first, separates coupled environmental and physiological drift offline, and links every output to cited NASA sources.

Why can’t deep-space crews rely on real-time Houston Mission Control?

One-way Mars communications delays reach up to 22 minutes (44 minutes round-trip), and solar conjunctions cut Earth contact altogether. When overlapping symptoms such as slower reaction time, tremor, or fatigue appear during a blackout, astronauts need autonomous onboard software that separates cabin environmental drift from physiological deconditioning and queues compact (<2 KB) store-and-forward packets for flight surgeons.

Space-to-Earth dual-use impact: The exact same zero-cloud camera kinematics, contactless rPPG, smartwatch telemetry bus, and offline triage workflow runs on commodity hardware for rural Texas clinics, austere disaster response, and home neuro-musculoskeletal tele-rehabilitation without broadband or cloud PHI exposure.

How does AstroKinetics · ASTRA go beyond a basic vitals dashboard?

It unifies six deterministic, inspectable mechanisms in one offline-capable workstation:

  • Gather locally (zero cloud video): Browser WebAssembly/WebGPU MediaPipe (478-pt FaceMesh Wang POS rPPG, 33-pt 3D WorldPose with 1-Euro filtering, 21-pt Hands tremor) plus a crew-isolated REST telemetry sandbox.
  • Earth-to-Orbit baseline adaptation: A 14-day personal baseline (224 readings across 16 measures) shifts expected ranges when entering microgravity, grounded in NASA’s Barger et al. (2014) ISS sleep norm (6.09 ± 0.67 h).
  • 8-DOF Mahalanobis whitening (Σ−1/2): Decomposes coupled multi-sensor drift into exact variance shares (% of DM2) and χ28 survival p-values with identical Python and offline JavaScript math.
  • Red-flag-first safety & abstention: Eight NASA HUNCH emergency rules run in <1 ms before quality gates; poor capture abstains (INCOMPLETE_CHECK_REQUIRED) instead of imputing normal defaults.
  • ASTRA episode governance & LiDAR volumetry: Enforces clinician-gated episode closure and computes exact closed-mesh signed tetrahedral limb volume while rejecting depth-hallucinating capture modes.
  • 3-tier crew privacy & 3D Crew Body Map: Redacts personal mood/journal logs for CMO and Flight Surgeon views both online and offline, and renders signals on BodyParts3D 4.0 anatomy (CC BY 4.0).

Which official NASA datasets and space-medicine standards power AstroKinetics · ASTRA?

Eight NASA and spaceflight resources are directly bundled or cited across the Evidence, Triage, and Workstation APIs — with explicit separation between human clinical guidance and model-organism research.

NASA OSDR OSD-571 (GLDS-563) · SpaceX Inspiration4 Plasma Proteomics
Bundled longitudinal measurements for 14 selected proteins across 4 crew members (C001–C004) and 6 timepoints (L-92, L-44, L-3, R+1, R+45, R+82) from 1,443 screened proteins, plus live REST metadata checks. Supplies spaceflight biological context; the readiness scorer uses a separate fixed demo reference. Inspect JSON (/api/osdr-empirical).
NASA OSDR RadLab · Bundled ISS DosTel1 & DosTel2 Radiation Sample
164 empirical ISS DosTel absorbed dose-rate (µGy/h), flux, orbital altitude, latitude/longitude, and L-shell observations captured from NASA’s RadLab sample window (2022-04-01 to 2022-04-02; DosTel1: 77 rows mean 6.595 µGy/h, DosTel2: 87 rows mean 5.948 µGy/h). Inspect JSON (/api/aura/radlab).
NASA-STD-3001, Volume 2, Revision F [V2 6004]
Requirement V2 6004 establishes the 3 mmHg one-hour mean habitat cabin CO₂ limit. The app explicitly labels its single-reading review thresholds (2.4 / 2.85 / 3.5 mmHg) as prototype snapshot settings.
Barger et al. (2014) NASA Space Shuttle & ISS Actigraphy Sleep Study (PMC4188436)
Published aggregate sleep-duration tables across SL-90, L-11, Inflight (ISS actigraphy 6.09 ± 0.67 h), R+7, and Night-before-EVA inform the Earth-1g vs. Mission-Microgravity sleep profile comparison in the HUNCH triage engine.
NASA Space Biology PMC Corpus (572 papers) & AURA Cited Library (673 total items)
Offline BM25 evidence engine indexing 572 NASA PMC publications, 70 reviewed space-medicine sources, 16 bilingual concepts, and 15 Apple Watch/iPhone clinical validation studies. Enforces human_only=true filtering and explicit caveats so rodent or cellular findings are never conflated with human clinical cutoffs. Search the Evidence tab · Corpus stats.
NASA NLSP Historic Manifest (AP005 Apollo HR & DSO 484 Circadian Shifting)
Cataloged with explicit reuse-limitation statements documenting why summarized historic tables provide physiological context rather than raw smartwatch training time series.
CSA / NASA ISS Bio-Monitor & NASA HUNCH Triage Frameworks
Informs the multi-channel wearable schema (HR, HRV RMSSD, SpO₂, respiration, wrist tremor) and the deterministic 8-red-flag safety gate. Inspect HUNCH catalog.
